About Us

BSP Fashion is a trusted name in the garment industry since 2017. We are a GST-registered manufacturer, wholesaler and trader specialising in high-quality Kids Wear, Men's Wear and Ladies Wear.

Based in Kolkata, we supply bulk orders across India with consistent quality, competitive pricing and reliable delivery through Delhivery, Expressbees, DTDC, India Post and transport services.
